# Halcyon, product context

Durable truth about what this is and who it is for. Visual decisions live in
[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md); the traps and conventions live in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).

## What Halcyon is

A consultancy in Chennai that builds bespoke operational software for small and
medium businesses: manufacturing, construction, contracting, industrial supply,
trading. Founded and run by **Sanjith Dhandapani**.

The offer is **operational intelligence**, not AI. Some systems use it where it
earns its place, and leading with it loses the audience, who are buying a
business that runs better rather than a technology.

## Who the visitor is

An owner or managing director of an established SME, typically 10 to 50 people.
They are not a technical buyer. They read spec sheets, rate cards, drawings and
GST invoices all day, and they judge a supplier on evidence rather than on
adjectives.

**They arrive with no mutual connection.** Halcyon takes clients from any source,
so nothing on the site may assume the reader already knows someone who has
worked with Halcyon. As of August 2026 the main channel is paid social:
Instagram and Facebook advertisements pointing either at this site or straight
at a WhatsApp conversation, decided per campaign.

They think in one of two ways, and both have to work:

- **By trade.** "We do pest control."
- **By the job that keeps coming back.** "We keep retyping quotations."

This is why search on `/works` spans trades as well as workflows, and why a
match on a trade Halcyon has never worked in is a first-class result.

## The honest position

- **One client: Swathi Engineering Agencies**, industrial flooring and coatings,
  with three systems. Two live, one being built.
- The site says so plainly. A page of logos would raise "who else?", which has
  no good answer yet, and the work stands up better than the roster does.
- The goal of every page is **a conversation with an owner**, not a follower and
  not brand awareness.

## What is being sold, underneath

Two workflows generalise past flooring and cover most Indian service SMEs:

- **Quoting.** A custom job is measured up, priced from a rate card, and turned
  into a sendable document. Interiors, signage, fabrication, printing, events.
- **Field work.** People go somewhere on your behalf, prove they were there, log
  it, and the owner finds out without ringing anyone. Pest control, servicing and
  AMC, solar, cleaning, security, catering.

Niche by **workflow**, not by industry. That decision is deliberate and settled.

## Hard content gates

These are not stylistic preferences. They come from Sanjith.

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Every figure comes from `halcyon-studio/brand/proof-ledger.md`, verbatim | One number that cannot be defended in a meeting costs more than every number that can. Never round, extrapolate, or convert to a percentage. |
| No invented clients, testimonials or outcomes | A visitor who works out that a claim was padded will not call. |
| Provenance stated on every system, never implied | `production` / `in-progress` / `demonstration`. Never promote a demonstration because it reads better. |
| No em dashes in visible copy | The one exception is the demos' own `DEMO — SAMPLE DATA` badge, quoted as it is. |
| "We", not "I" | Halcyon is a company. First person survives only where Sanjith personally does the thing. |
| Never the word "free" | Use "no cost", or "included". |
| Never lead with AI | The offer is the operation running better. |
| No real client data anywhere in a demo | Every company, person, price and figure inside them is invented. |

## What success looks like on this site

A stranger who arrived from an advertisement sees working software inside ten
seconds, finds their own trade in the search field, opens an application and
uses it, and leaves able to reach Sanjith. Nothing on the site is a WhatsApp
button; the advertisement decides the destination and the site behaves like a
website.
